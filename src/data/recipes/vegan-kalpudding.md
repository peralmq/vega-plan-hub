## Ingredients

| quantity | unit | key             | ingredient                     | notes                                   |
|----------|------|-----------------|--------------------------------|-----------------------------------------|
| 1        |      | cabbage         | white cabbage head             | about 1 kg, shredded                    |
| 3        | tbsp | margarine       | dairy-free margarine           | for caramelizing                        |
| 1        | dl   | golden-syrup    | golden syrup (ljus sirap)      | divided: most in the cabbage ✨         |
| 400      | g    | vegan-mince     | vegan ground mince             | e.g. Anamma formbar färs                |
| 1        |      | onion           | yellow onion, finely chopped   |                                         |
| 1        | dl   | oat-cream       | oat cream                      | for the mince mix                       |
| 0.75     | dl   | breadcrumbs     | breadcrumbs                    | for the mince mix                       |
| 1        | tbsp | soy-sauce       | soy sauce                      | for the mince mix                       |
| 0.5      | tsp  | allspice        | ground allspice                | kryddpeppar                             |
| 1        | tsp  | salt            | salt                           | divided over cabbage and mince          |
| 0.5      | tsp  | black-pepper    | black pepper                   |                                         |
| 2        | dl   | vegetable-broth | vegetable broth                | for the oven dish                       |
| 800      | g    | potatoes        | potatoes, boiled               | to serve                                |
| 1        | dl   | lingonberry-jam | lingonberry jam                | to serve 🫐                             |

## Instructions

1. Set the oven to 200 °C. Shred the cabbage into ribbons, core discarded.
2. Caramelize the cabbage: melt the margarine in your largest pot and cook the cabbage over medium-high heat, stirring now and then, 15–20 minutes until soft and golden at the edges. Work in two batches if it won't fit — it shrinks fast.
3. Drizzle in about ¾ of the syrup and half the salt and keep cooking 5 more minutes until glossy and deeply golden brown. This caramelization is the entire soul of kålpudding — don't rush it. ✨
4. Mix the mince: soak the breadcrumbs in the oat cream a couple of minutes, then mix in the mince, chopped onion, soy sauce, allspice, the rest of the salt, and the pepper.
5. Layer in a greased oven dish: half the cabbage, all the mince pressed into an even layer, then the rest of the cabbage on top. Drizzle the top with the last of the syrup and pour the broth in around the edges.
6. Bake in the middle of the oven for 40–45 minutes until the top is dark golden and the edges bubble. Let it rest 10 minutes — it slices instead of slumps. 🥬
7. Serve squares of kålpudding with boiled potatoes, a big spoon of lingonberry jam, and the pan juices spooned over. 🫐✨

## Notes

- The pan juices are the traditional "gravy" — spoon them straight from the dish. Sauce-lovers can make the brunsås from the vegan meatballs recipe alongside.
- Batch dream: bakes ahead beautifully, reheats better than fresh, and freezes in portions for 3 months. This is why the dish serves 6.
- Dark edges on the cabbage are flavor, not failure — caramelized-to-almost-burnt is exactly how mormor made it.
- Kid note: it's basically cabbage candy with meatball filling — the syrup wins over skeptics.
