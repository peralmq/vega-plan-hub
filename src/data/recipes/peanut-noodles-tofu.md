## Ingredients

| quantity | unit | key           | ingredient                       | notes                              |
|----------|------|---------------|----------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| 400      | g    | tofu          | extra-firm tofu                  | or vegan chicken bits              |
| 2        | tbsp | cornstarch    | cornstarch                       | for the crispy coating             |
| 3        | tbsp | oil           | neutral oil                      | for frying                         |
| 3        | tbsp | soy-sauce     | soy sauce                        | 1 for the tofu, 2 for the sauce    |
| 300      | g    | noodles       | noodles                          | wheat, ramen, or udon              |
| 1        | dl   | peanut-butter | peanut butter                    | smooth, for the sauce 🥜           |
| 1        | tbsp | rice-vinegar  | rice vinegar                     | or lime juice, for the sauce       |
| 2        | tbsp | maple-syrup   | maple syrup or sugar             | for the sauce                      |
| 1        | tsp  | sesame-oil    | toasted sesame oil               | for the sauce                      |

| 1        | tbsp | sriracha      | sriracha                         | optional, on the side              |

## Instructions

1. Press the tofu briefly between paper towels, tear or cut it into bite-sized pieces, and toss with 1 tbsp soy sauce, then the cornstarch until coated.
2. Fry the tofu in the oil over medium-high heat, undisturbed 2–3 minutes per side, until golden and crispy all over, about 10 minutes total. (Vegan chicken: just fry per the package.) 🔥
3. Meanwhile, whisk the sauce: peanut butter, 2 tbsp soy sauce, rice vinegar, maple syrup, sesame oil, garlic, and ginger, thinned with the hot water until pourable like cream.
4. Cook the noodles according to the package, drain, and save a cup of the cooking water.
5. Toss the hot noodles with the peanut sauce in the pot, loosening with a splash of noodle water until every strand is glossy. 🍜
6. Top with the crispy tofu, cucumber, spring onions, sesame seeds, and peanuts. Sriracha on the side for those who want the jordnötsnudlar to bite back. 🥜✨

## Notes

- The sauce is deliberately mild and a little sweet — peak kid appeal; the heat lives in the sriracha bottle on the table.
- Sauce thickens as it cools: leftover-lunch portions want an extra splash of water when reheating (or eat them cold — cold peanut noodles are a delicacy).
- Tearing the tofu instead of cubing gives craggy edges that crisp better and hold more sauce.
