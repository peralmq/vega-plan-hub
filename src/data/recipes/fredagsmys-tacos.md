## Instructions

1. Stir together the spice mix in a small bowl: chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, oregano, salt, and sugar. (Double it and jar the rest — future Friday-you says thanks.) ✨
2. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat and fry the mince for 4–5 minutes until it starts to brown.
3. Add the tomato paste and the spice mix and fry for 1 minute until fragrant, then pour in the water. Simmer for 5 minutes until saucy and glossy. Taste for salt.
4. Meanwhile, chop the topping bar: tomatoes, cucumber, bell pepper, corn, lettuce, cheese, crème fraîche, avocado, and salsa — each in its own little bowl on the table. 🥗
5. Warm the tortillas in a dry frying pan 20–30 seconds per side (or taco shells in the oven at 175 °C for 3–4 minutes).
6. Put everything on the table and let everyone build their own. Fredagsmys! 🌮🎉

## Notes

- The mince simmer is mild on purpose — the hot sauce bottle on the table is where the heat lives, so kids and chili-heads share one dinner.
- The spice mix replaces a store-bought taco kit; it keeps for months in a jar, so make a triple batch.
- Leftover taco mince is great in wraps or on a loaded baked potato the next day.
