## Ingredients

## Instructions

1. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a heavy pot over high heat and brown the oumph hard, 4–5 minutes, until it has real color. Set aside — the browning is where the "boeuf" flavor lives. 🔥
2. In the same pot with another 1 tbsp oil, brown the mushrooms undisturbed until golden, 5 minutes. Set aside with the oumph.
3. Lower to medium, add the last of the oil, and cook the pearl onions and carrots until the onions take on color at the edges, 5 minutes. Add the garlic and tomato paste, cook 1 minute, then sprinkle over the flour and stir it in.
4. Pour in the red wine, scraping up every browned bit from the bottom, and let it bubble and reduce by half, about 5 minutes.
5. Add the broth, soy sauce, bay leaves, and thyme. Return the oumph (not the mushrooms yet), cover ajar, and simmer gently for 30 minutes, stirring now and then. 🍷
6. Meanwhile, boil and mash the potatoes with a knob of margarine, salt, and a splash of oat milk or cooking water.
7. Stir the mushrooms back in and simmer uncovered 10 more minutes until the sauce is glossy and coats a spoon. Fish out the bay leaves and thyme stems, swirl in the margarine, and season with salt and pepper.
8. Serve over the mash, showered with parsley. Bourguignon sans boeuf. 🍄✨

## Notes

- Most of the alcohol simmers off over the 40 minutes, and the flavor mellows — but for a fully alcohol-free pot, swap the wine for 3 dl extra broth plus 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar.
- Batch hero: it is genuinely better on day two, keeps 4 days in the fridge, and freezes for 3 months — make a double pot.
- Frozen pearl onions (pre-peeled) are a shortcut with zero shame and zero tears.
- Many red wines are filtered with animal products; Systembolaget marks vegan wines with a symbol — check the shelf tag.
